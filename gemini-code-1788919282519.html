Representing Kochi’s Culture & Supporting Our Neighborhoods

Kochi Local is a Section 8 non-profit initiative dedicated to preserving local history, amplifying community voices, and building inclusive public spaces across Greater Kochi.

Our Strategic Pillars

Empowering communities through storytelling, advocacy, and urban development.

01

Media & Cultural Advocacy

Documenting Kochi’s unseen history, culture, and community narratives across local neighborhoods.

02

Grassroots Urban Renewal

Transforming overlooked spaces into safe public community grounds for children and elders.

03

Intergenerational Spaces

Hosting free cultural, arts, and sports gatherings to bridge generational divides in the city.

Flagship Initiative

Anandapuram Inclusive Community Park

A dedicated project to convert under-utilized land in Anandapuram into a multi-purpose park for local children and senior citizens, serving as a scalable model for underrepresented urban areas.
